Wilds

Chop trees, mix recipes, build a camp and last the night. Everything is earned by playing. There is nothing to buy.

Keyboard & mouse

  • WASD or arrows to move
  • Space or click to hit, eat, or place
  • 1–8 or scroll to pick what you hold
  • E to open your bag, Esc to pause

Touch

  • Drag on the left side to move
  • Tap A or the right side to act
  • Tap the hotbar to pick what you hold

Tips

  • Whatever you hold decides what A does: swing it, eat it, or place it.
  • Recipes are secret. Put ingredients in the mixing pot to find them.
  • Monsters come out at night. They won't appear near a campfire, but ghouls can break walls.
  • Slimes and spiders sometimes drop eggs. Hatch one for a pet that fights with you.
  • Every 4th night the Stone Golem wakes up.
