# PSG2 Screenshot Notes

What Brenden's screenshots and recordings of the real game showed. The images themselves aren't stored here, because they're the original game's art.

## 2026-10-17: 12 screenshots
- **HUD:**
  - gems sit right of the clock (`+` 💎 count in green-cyan);
  - no day label outside Survival;
  - no coin counter on the HUD.
- **Town street, left to right:** a dark-haired girl, the Plumber (red cap) with "New stocked items!", the player, **Banka** (blonde pigtails tied with red, purple-pink dress), then **the spiky-haired blond seller** (grey shirt, dark pants). That's the Merchant.
- **Bag screen:**
  - **6 backpack tabs** across the top;
  - a 5 × 6 storage grid next to the 5 × 5 bag;
  - trash and X buttons on the left.
- **Gatekeeper:** world cards two at a time on a dark board inside an orange frame, an X button outside at the bottom right, and the titles and texts listed in [[Worlds]].

## 2026-10-17: screenshot plus a 16-second recording (Darklands-like world)
- **Three small orange square buttons right of the bars**: friend (person +), chat (bubble), emotes.
- The **Chat..** text sits under the bars.
- **Emote panel**: an orange frame with a peach inside, 7 × 3, in the order listed in [[Emotes]].
- **Damage numbers:** big, white or grey with dark outlines, stacking quickly during fast swings.
- **Death:** purple puffs.
- **Dark vignette:** the edges of the screen fade to black in that world.
- The player wore a rainbow cape and blue wings, and the name showed in red (a tag like `[BROKEN] The Lost One`).
- Backgrounds were dark crisscross brick walls with mossy edges.
