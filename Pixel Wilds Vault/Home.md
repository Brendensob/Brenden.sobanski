# Pixel Wilds: Home

Pixel Wilds is a Godot 4.3 game that plays like **Pixel Survival Game 2 (PSG2)**. Every sprite and sound is made in code, and it may go to Steam later. This vault is the project's memory. Brenden reads it in Obsidian, and Claude reads it at the start of every session and updates it after every change.
