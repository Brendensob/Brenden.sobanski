# HUD and Controls

Laid out like Brenden's PSG2 screenshots.

![[hud_emotes.png]]

## Screen
- **Top left:** 5 peach hotbar slots (green border on the one you're holding).
- **Under them:** the red, blue and green bars (health, mana, stamina).
- **Right of the bars:** three orange buttons, top to bottom:
  - **friends**;
  - **chat** (offline it says chat needs online play);
  - **emotes** (see [[Emotes]]).
- **Under the bars:** the **Chat..** box (only in online rooms), then the **goal card**: what to do next, with progress. Tap it for how (see [[Goals and Guidance]]).
- **Top centre:** the turning sky clock, with **gems** next to it (`+` 💎 count). Tap them for the Gem Shop.
- **Day counter:** only in Survival Grasslands.
- **Right side:** backpack (bag), compass (menu), bomb (back to town).
- **Bottom:** grey ◀ ▶ bottom left; round red **A** above round green **B** bottom right.
- **Low health:** under 25%, the health bar's border pulses red.
- **Ready notices:** "Your Copper Bar is ready in the furnace", "…has hatched", "…is ripe", shown wherever you are.
- Your name shows in red over your head. Pickups read "Wood obtained" under the clock.
- Coins are on the bag's character page, not on the HUD.

## Menus and messages (2026-10-27)

![[clean_dialog.png]]

- **Rounded cartoon boxes:** menus have a bold orange rim, rounded corners and a soft drop shadow. Buttons have a darker lip at the bottom and sink when pressed. Item slots are rounded too.
- **Menus pop in** with a quick grow, and the game behind them is softly dimmed (only lightly for villager talk).
- **Tap outside a menu to close it**, for every menu with an ✕ button.
- **Messages** ("Wood obtained", "Pixel Town") sit in small dark bubbles under the clock. The same message again counts up ("x3") instead of stacking, only three show at once, and they clear when a menu opens.
- **Bars** are rounded with a shiny top.
- **Touch buttons:** round red **A** and green **B** with a shine and a shadow, and grey ◀ ▶ pads with arrows. They sink when pressed.
- A villager's greeting sits a line above your own name, so the two never overlap. Names fade while you stand on top of a villager.

## Controls
| | Keyboard | Phone or mouse |
|---|---|---|
| Move | A / D or arrows | ◀ ▶ |
| Jump (B) | Space, W, K or Up | B |
| Hit / use (A) | J, X, Enter, or click anywhere | A |
| Bag | E | Backpack |
| Hotbar slot | 1–5 | Tap the slot |
| Menu | Esc | Compass |
| Chat | T | Chat.. box |
| Back to town | | Bomb |
| Auto hit on/off | Q | The little round **AUTO** button left of A |

## Auto hit (2026-10-27)

![[auto_hit.png]]

A little round button just left of A, with a sword on it and "AUTO" under it. It's grey when off and gold when on. *Brenden: "Keep the buttons how they were just for the movement and jump also add a little circle button for auto hit to turn on and off so your sword swings automatically even while in inv and stays even while you switch items"*
- **On:** the weapon or tool you hold swings by itself, over and over. That covers swords, axes, pickaxes, staffs, bows, throwables and bare hands. Food and blocks are never used by themselves.
- It **keeps swinging with the bag open**: while auto hit is on, the bag doesn't pause the world.
- It **stays on** when you press A, switch items or faint. Only the button (or Q) turns it off.
- It's saved on the device, like the sound settings.
- The original's trick still works: holding A while you open the bag turns auto hit on.
- The move and jump buttons are unchanged.
