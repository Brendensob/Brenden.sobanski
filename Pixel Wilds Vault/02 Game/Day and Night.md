# Day and Night

- A day lasts **216 s**: 99 s of daytime, 45 s of sunset, 72 s of night, then straight back to morning.
- Coming into Pixel Town from the menu always starts in the morning.
- The sky clock at the top turns with the time.
- The **day number only shows in Survival Grasslands**. Brenden: "no one really cares for it".
