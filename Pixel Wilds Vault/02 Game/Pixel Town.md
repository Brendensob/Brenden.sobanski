# Pixel Town

Small and closed in on all sides (a roof on top), with three floors. Since 2026-10-27 the roof and outer walls are drawn as mossy stone, the two room walls as stone brick, and the basement has a stone-brick back wall and a cobbled floor (see [[Art Style]]). The layout comes from Brenden's sketch (2026-10-09) and his screenshots (2026-10-17). In code it's `level.gd` → the town generator. The map is **64 × 28 tiles**.

## Up top (west → east)
- **Trading Center**: a closed room behind a wooden wall. Burn the wall with a Torch. Inside are the Trader and two trading tables (see [[Multiplayer]]).
- **Incubator**, and the **Silver, Golden and Master** chests, each with a signboard.
- **Furnace room**: mirrors the Trading Center. The green GateKeeper's gate is its door (the Pretzel quest).
- The way up from the street is an **opening in the exact middle**. Jump up through it: a jump plus two jumps in the air.

## The street (west → east)
1. Magic soils in a cave behind a rock wall (break it with a gold pickaxe)
2. Miner, then Brutus
3. Miffie (yellow dress)
4. The sign
5. The open space under the opening: the **Crafter** on its left edge, the **portal** in the exact middle (right under the opening's middle, so the two line up), and the **Gatekeeper** on its right edge, mirroring the Crafter
6. Plumber → **Banka** (storage) → **Merchant** (spiky blond hair)

![[portal_area.png]]

That order matches Brenden's screenshot (Plumber, Banka, then the spiky-haired seller). The Crafter moved from the east end to beside the portal on 2026-10-29, when Brenden asked for it. The four houses mirror around the middle. See [[NPCs]].

## The basement
- A massive stone wall at the east end of the street blocks the way down. Smash it with the Wall Hammer, earned by surviving 10 nights in Survival Grasslands (the night number is a guess).
- The ninjas Nini, Nana and Nina upgrade the Tsurugi. The robots FC 9912, TT 1001 and 2219 OOP upgrade the Iron Fist.
