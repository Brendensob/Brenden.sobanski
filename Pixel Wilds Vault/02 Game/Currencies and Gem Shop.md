# Currencies and Gem Shop

- **Pixel Coins:** from selling, quests and drops. Shown on the bag's character page.
- **Survival Tokens:** from surviving nights in Survival Grasslands. The Miner takes them.
- **Gems:** shown next to the clock in cyan. Tap them for the Gem Shop.

## Gem Shop prices
| Item | Gems |
|---|---|
| 3 Silver / Golden / Master Keys | 5 / 15 / 40 |
| Characters | 100, 300, 800 or 2,000 (the original's tiers) |
| Combination Scrolls, Magic Seeds | estimates |
| 1 gem | 2,500 Pixel Coins (in place of real-money packs) |

**Where gems come from:**
- bosses (1–2, rarely);
- Golden and Master Chests;
- Red and Golden Magic Seeds;
- Miffie's quests and daily bounty;
- the GateKeeper's mask quest.

Plans for real money are in [[Monetization]].
