# Crafting and Combining

## The Crafter
- Five tabs: weapons and tools, helmets, armor, shields, rings.
- Pick an item to see it big, with its stats and the three things it takes (have / need, green when you have enough), then **Craft**.
- He has every recipe the PSG2 wiki lists a Crafter cost for, including:
  - the Hell Armor line, Nightmare, Emperor, Skull, Snow and Dragon dresses;
  - the rings;
  - hats made from characters.

## Combining (in the bag)
- Put 2 or 3 items in the slots.
- Each recipe has a base success chance. Having its Combo Book in your bag adds 50%, and a Combination Scroll adds 35%.
- A failed or unknown combination gives Dust.
- **Combo books:** I–V, Survival, Z (28 recipes), ZX (22) and U (14). Missing Pages drop from late bosses.

## Furnaces and smelting
Furnaces are upstairs in [[Pixel Town]]. They smelt bars: Sandnite Bars (5 Sandnite Ore + Coal), Forbidden Bars and more.

All recipes are in `scripts/data.gd`.
