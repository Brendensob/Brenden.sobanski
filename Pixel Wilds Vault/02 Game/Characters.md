# Characters

- **Starting characters:** Man in Suit or Nurse.
- **Create a character shows all 18.** Locked ones are dark with a padlock. Tap one to see its stats and how to unlock it. Unlocks count for every save on the device.

![[create_character_unlocks.png]]

| Character | Unlocks by (or gems) |
|---|---|
| Cavemun | Reach day 7 in Survival Grasslands (100) |
| Backstreet Boy | Clear the Grasslands Arena (100) |
| Pirate | Miffie's 99 Scarabs quest (100) |
| Bad Man | Miffie's 99 Stink Bugs quest (100) |
| School Girl | Clear the Darklands Arena (100) |
| Soldier | Miffie's 99 Fire Bugs quest (300) |
| The Spi | Clear the Hell Arena (300) |
| ChuChu | Reach day 19 in Survival Grasslands (300) |
| Sailor Moons | Clear the Dream Arena (300) |
| Q, Mad and Nerd Bun | Gems only (300) |
| Dark Knight | Clear the Ghost Arena (800) |
| Drone | Reach day 45 in Survival Grasslands (800) |
| Ninja, Iron Bot | Gems only (2000) |

- **Any other way works too:** getting the character from a chest, a monster or the Gem Shop unlocks it. A finished task also puts one in your bag.
- **Unlock with gems** right on the screen: it uses the gems of your saved character that has the most.
- Starting as a character with a weapon (Cavemun, Pirate, The Spi, Ninja, Iron Bot) gives you that weapon.
- **Found ones:** Cavemun, Pirate, The Spi, Bad Man, School Girl, Soldier, ChuChu, Drone, Dark Knight, Backstreet Boy and Sailor Moons drop from monsters, bosses and chests.
- **In the Gem Shop:** every character, including the Ninja, the Iron Bot and the Q, Mad and Nerd Buns.
- Each character adds its own attack, defense, health and mana. Some also give a weapon:
  - Cavemun → Wood King;
  - Pirate → Golden Night;
  - The Spi → Blue Night;
  - Ninja → Tsurugi;
  - Iron Bot → Iron Fist.
![[become_ask.png]]

- **Switching asks first:** "Become the Pirate?" explains that your current character goes back in your bag and your bag, gear and coins stay. **Cancel** changes nothing. Using the character you already are does nothing (the item isn't wasted).
- **Switching character** gives your old character back as an item, so nothing is lost. A character's bonus weapon comes only the first time you become it.
- The Crafter turns characters into hats.

> [!warning]
> The character stats come from the 2.o game, because PSG2's aren't published. See [[Known Issues]].
