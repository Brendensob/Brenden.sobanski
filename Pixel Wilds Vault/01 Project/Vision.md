# Vision

**Pixel Wilds** is a side-scrolling pixel survival-crafting game that plays like **Pixel Survival Game 2** (by Cowbeans), made in **Godot 4.3**.

- **Goal:** feel like PSG2 (the same items, recipes, characters, worlds, town, controls and HUD), with our own art drawn in code.
- **Ambition:** possibly ship on **Steam**, and earn money from it (see [[Monetization]]).
- **Look:** pixel art with a cartoon twist (see [[Art Style]]).
- **Platforms now:**
  - PC through Godot;
  - phones and any browser through the web build (see [[Running and Building]]).
- **Multiplayer:** friends-only rooms of up to 4 through a relay server (see [[Multiplayer]]).

## What "like PSG2" means here
- Match what the [[PSG2 Sources|PSG2 wiki]], the official trailer and Brenden's own screenshots show.
- Where nothing is published (drop rates, many stats), make a reasonable estimate and log it in [[Numbers and Estimates]].
- Don't copy the original's sprites, sounds or code. Draw and synthesize our own.

## The other version
The repo also holds an older top-down browser game (root `index.html`, `js/`). It lives at `/classic/` on GitHub Pages and isn't being worked on.
